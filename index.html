🌾 Dharanika Core Features

Intelligent tools designed for farmers

1. 🌱 AI Personalised Crop Advisor

Get suitable crop recommendations based on soil, season, water availability and farming conditions.

2. 🧠 AI Farm Decision Simulator

Explore what-if farming scenarios involving crop, water, budget, investment, risk and revenue.

3. 🌿 AI Plant Health Scanner

Upload a plant image and get a basic plant-health analysis interface with possible issues and solutions.

4. 🌍 AI Soil Health & Organic Farming Planner

Check soil health information and receive organic fertilizer and crop-planning suggestions.

5. 📈 AI Market Intelligence

Understand market trends, demand and possible selling-time strategies.

6. 🛒 Farmer Marketplace

A future direct farmer-to-customer marketplace for selling farm products.

7. 🤖 AI Farming Assistant

Ask farming questions using text or voice in local languages.

8. 💧 AI Irrigation Optimizer

Calculate approximate water requirements and create a simple irrigation plan.

9. 🌦️ AI Weather & Crop Alerts

Use your location to get current weather information and farming-related alerts.

10. 🐛 AI Pest Early-Warning System

Identify potential pest risks based on crop and weather conditions.

11. 🔄 AI Crop Rotation Planner

Get simple crop rotation suggestions based on the previous crop.

12. 🌾 AI Harvest Prediction

Estimate approximate harvest readiness using crop and growing-period information.

13. 📊 AI Farm Record Management

Track crop expenses, yield, income and farm profit.

14. ⭐ Dharanika Trust Score

A future reliability score based on seller verification and customer feedback.

15. 🧬 Farm Memory / Digital Farm Profile

Store farm history to provide more personalised recommendations in future versions.
